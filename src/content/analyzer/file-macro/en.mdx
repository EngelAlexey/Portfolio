## What it means

The Office document carries instructions that can run when it is opened. One form is macros, which are small programs stored inside the document. Another is a DDE field, an old Office function able to launch a program on the computer.

## What the risk is

A macro or a DDE field can install a harmful program on your computer. Office asks for permission before running them, and attackers ask for it with texts such as "Enable content to view the document".

## What to do

Do not click "Enable content" and do not agree to update links or fields. If you expected the document, confirm with the sender, through another channel, that it should have macros.
