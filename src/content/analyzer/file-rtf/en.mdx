## What it means

The RTF document carries an embedded object, which is another file or program stored inside the document.

## What the risk is

Attackers use the objects of RTF documents to take advantage of flaws in Word and run a program on your computer. It can happen when the document is opened, without clicking anything else. A normal RTF document almost never carries objects.

## What to do

Do not open the document. If you expected it, ask the sender to send it as a PDF. Keep Word and Windows up to date.
