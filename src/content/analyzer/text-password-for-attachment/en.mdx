## What it means

The message gives a password to open an attached file.

## What the risk is

A password-protected file stops automatic analysis from seeing its content. MITRE describes attackers who use this technique to deliver phishing files.

## What to do

Do not open the file. Confirm with the sender, through another channel, that they sent it and that it should carry a password.
