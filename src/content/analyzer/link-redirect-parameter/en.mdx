## What it means

The address contains another address inside a parameter, such as `?url=` or `?redirect=`. The site of the first address redirects to the second.

## What the risk is

The site of the first address can be legitimate and still lead somewhere else. An attacker uses it so that the link looks like it belongs to a trusted site.

## What to do

Read the second address: that is where the link goes. If you do not recognise it, do not click.
