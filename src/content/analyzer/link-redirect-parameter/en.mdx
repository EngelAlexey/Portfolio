## What it means

The address contains another address written inside it, such as `?url=` or `?redirect=`. The first site sends the person on to the second.

## What the risk is

The first site can be legitimate and still lead somewhere else. An attacker uses it so that the link looks like it belongs to a trusted site.

## What to do

Read the second address: that is where the link goes. If you do not recognise it, do not click.
