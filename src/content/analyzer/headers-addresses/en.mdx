## What it means

The address that replies go to, or the address that error notices go to, belongs to a site different from the sender's.

## What the risk is

If you reply, the reply goes to someone else. Legitimate bulk mailings also use another site for error notices.

## What to do

Before replying, check which address your reply goes to.
