## What it means

The site name includes letters from other alphabets, or mixes alphabets in one word, such as a Cyrillic "а" inside a word written in Latin letters. Browsers store those names with the prefix `xn--`.

## What the risk is

Letters from different alphabets can look identical. So a name can look like a brand without being one. A name with `xn--` can also be legitimate, for example if it has accented letters.

## What to do

If you do not recognise the site, do not click the link. Type the address yourself.
