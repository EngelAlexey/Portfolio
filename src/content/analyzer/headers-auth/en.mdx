## What it means

The technical data of the email says whether the server that received it could check that it is authentic. Those checks are called SPF, DKIM and DMARC. A `fail` or a `softfail` means the sender's site did not authorise that sending. A digital signature from another site means the signature does not belong to the sender.

## What the risk is

An email that fails these checks can be forged. A `pass` does not prove that the email is legitimate.

Only the first `Authentication-Results` field was added by your own mail server. Anyone can write the others.

## What to do

Take the result as one more hint. If in doubt, confirm the message through another channel.

If the technical data has no such field, the authenticity of the email cannot be read.
