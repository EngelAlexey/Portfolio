## What it means

The headers say whether the server that received the message could check SPF, DKIM and DMARC. A `fail` or a `softfail` means the domain did not authorise that sending. A misaligned DKIM signature belongs to a domain different from the sender's.

## What the risk is

A message that fails these checks can be forged. A `pass` does not prove that the message is legitimate.

Only the topmost `Authentication-Results` field was added by your own server. Anyone can write the others.

## What to do

Take the result as one more hint. If in doubt, confirm the message through another channel.

Without that field the authentication cannot be read.
