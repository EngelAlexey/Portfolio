## What it means

The displayed name does not match the real address. It names a brand from another domain, shows a different address or is a brand writing from a free mailbox.

## What the risk is

The sender writes the visible name and nobody checks it.

## What to do

Look at the full address and not only the name. A company usually writes from its own domain.
