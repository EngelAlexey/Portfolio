## What it means

The message asks for payment by a means such as gift cards, cryptocurrency, an urgent transfer or SINPE Móvil, or it mentions a pending payment.

## What the risk is

These means of payment are usually hard to reverse.

## What to do

Do not pay. Confirm the debt through an official channel.

If you already paid, tell your bank or whoever issued the payment straight away.
