## What it means

The times of the mail journey, which each server records in the `Received` field, do not add up:

- a step is earlier than the one before it;
- there is a long delay;
- the date of the message is far from the first step.

## What the risk is

It can indicate a message that was held or forwarded. It also happens in legitimate mail with failures or queues.

## What to do

Take it as a weak signal. Give it more weight if it appears together with others.
