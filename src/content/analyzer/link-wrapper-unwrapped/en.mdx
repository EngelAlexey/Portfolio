## What it means

The link first goes through a mail protection service, such as SafeLinks or Proofpoint. This analysis reads the real address inside, without opening anything.

## What the risk is

The address of the service is legitimate and says nothing about the destination. According to Microsoft, a link can be legitimate on delivery and harmful later.

## What to do

Read the destination address and the signals for that destination.
