## What it means

The message asks you to verify the account or to give a password, a verification code or card details.

## What the risk is

With that data someone can enter your account or use your card. Companies do not usually ask for a password or a verification code by message.

## What to do

Do not reply or type any data. Enter your account by typing the address yourself.

If you already gave data, change the password and tell your bank.
