## What it means

The message or a page asks you to open `Win+R` or a terminal, paste a command and run it. The technique is called ClickFix.

## What the risk is

A pasted command runs with your permissions and goes around the defences of the browser and the mail. Microsoft documented this technique for installing programs that steal information.

## What to do

Do not paste or run anything. Close the page.

If you already did, disconnect the computer from the Internet and change your passwords from another device.
