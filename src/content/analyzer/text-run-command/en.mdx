## What it means

The message or a page asks you to open a Windows window with the Windows and R keys (`Win+R`) or a terminal. Then it asks you to paste some text and run it. This technique is called ClickFix.

## What the risk is

Text pasted this way runs with your permissions and goes around the defences of the browser and the mail. Microsoft documented this technique for installing programs that steal information.

## What to do

Do not paste or run anything. Close the page.

If you already did, disconnect the computer from the Internet and change your passwords from another device.
