## What it means

The address has an unusual form for a company site:

- an IP address instead of a name;
- a port that is not the usual one;
- `http` without encryption;
- many subdomains;
- a long name that looks random.

## What the risk is

Legitimate company sites usually use their own domain, HTTPS and the usual port. These forms are less common on them. One of them alone proves nothing.

## What to do

If the message says it comes from a company, go in through the address you know and not through the link.
