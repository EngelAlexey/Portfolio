## What it means

The address has an unusual form for a company site:

- numbers instead of the name of a site;
- an extra number after the name, such as `:8080`;
- an unencrypted connection, that is, without a padlock;
- many parts before the name of the site;
- a long name that looks random.

## What the risk is

The sites of legitimate companies usually have their own name, an encrypted connection and a simple address. One of these forms proves nothing on its own.

## What to do

If the message says it comes from a company, go in through the address you know and not through the link.
