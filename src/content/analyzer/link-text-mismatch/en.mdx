## What it means

The text of the link shows one address and the link goes to another.

## What the risk is

Whoever writes the message decides the text of the link and nobody checks it. Seeing a familiar address does not guarantee that the link goes there.

## What to do

Do not click the link. If you want to visit that site, type the address into the browser yourself.
