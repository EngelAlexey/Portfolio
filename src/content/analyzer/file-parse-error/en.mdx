## What it means

This analysis could not read all the content of the file. It may be damaged, too large or complex, or made to confuse review programs.

## What the risk is

What this analysis could not read was not checked. The result may be incomplete.

## What to do

Treat the file as unchecked. If you did not expect it, do not open it. If you did, confirm with the sender, through another channel, that they sent it.
