## What it means

The address has a name before an at sign, such as `paypal.com@site.example`. What comes before the at sign is a user. The site is what comes after.

The address standard (RFC 3986) calls that part "userinfo".

## What the risk is

Putting a familiar name first makes the link look as if it belongs to that company.

## What to do

Read the address from the at sign to the right. Do not click the link if that site is not the one you expect.
