## What it means

The address has a name before an at sign, such as `paypal.com@site.example`. What comes before the at sign is not the site. The real site is what comes after.

## What the risk is

A familiar name at the start makes the link look as if it belongs to that company, even though it leads to another site.

## What to do

Read the address from the at sign to the right. If that site is not the one you expect, do not click the link.
