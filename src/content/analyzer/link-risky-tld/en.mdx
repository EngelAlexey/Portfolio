## What it means

The ending of the address, such as `.zip`, `.top` or `.click`, is on a list of endings that are often used for abuse.

## What the risk is

Many legitimate sites use those endings. Reputation reports, such as those from Spamhaus, point out some of them, such as `.top`. The list in this analysis is an estimate and not an official classification. On its own, the ending is a weak signal.

## What to do

Give it more weight if it appears together with other signals.
