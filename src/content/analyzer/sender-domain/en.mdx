## What it means

The site name in the sender address looks like that of a well-known brand or mixes alphabets.

## What the risk is

Anyone can write an address that looks like a company's. A similar address does not prove that the message comes from it.

## What to do

Do not reply or click links. Type the official address of the company yourself to get in touch.
