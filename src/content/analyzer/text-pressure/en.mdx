## What it means

The text uses typical pressure phrases: haste, a threat, a prize, an authority, secrecy or a greeting that does not use your name.

## What the risk is

These phrases aim to make you act before you think. Legitimate messages can also use them, so they are a weak signal and not proof.

## What to do

Stop and verify through another channel. Call the official number of the company and not the one in the message.
