## What it means

The address includes an email address, sometimes encoded.

## What the risk is

The link is made for whoever receives it, because it carries their address. Legitimate bulk messages also do this, for example to unsubscribe or to confirm a subscription.

## What to do

Do not sign in from that link. Go in through the official address of the site.
