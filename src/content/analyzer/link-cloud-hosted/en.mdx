## What it means

The link goes to a hosting or shared-document service, such as a free site, cloud storage or a form. The domain belongs to the service, not to the company that signs the message.

## What the risk is

Anyone can publish content on those services, so a well-known domain does not guarantee that the content is legitimate. Attackers use them to blend in with expected traffic.

## What to do

Check who shared the link and through which channel. Do not type passwords or personal data into a form you did not expect.
