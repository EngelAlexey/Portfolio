## What it means

The link goes to a service where anyone can publish pages, files or forms, such as a free site, online storage or a form. The site name belongs to the service and not to the company that signs the message.

## What the risk is

A well-known name does not guarantee that the content is legitimate, because anyone can publish there. Attackers use these services to go unnoticed.

## What to do

Confirm with the person who sent you the link that they sent it and why. Do not type passwords or personal data into a form you did not expect.
